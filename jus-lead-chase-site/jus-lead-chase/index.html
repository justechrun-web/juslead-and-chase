Built for the big moves

Escort support for carriers and dispatchers moving permitted oversize loads, from route review before the move to escort on the day.

  • Lead & chase escorts

    Front and rear pilot vehicles that warn traffic, hold lanes where permitted and keep the driver informed by radio through every turn and pinch point.

    Request lead and chase escort

  • High-pole services

    A measured height pole ahead of tall loads to check bridges, signals, wires and other overhead clearances before the load reaches them.

    Request high-pole service

  • Route surveys

    Pre-move reconnaissance of the permitted route: turning radii, bridge and overhead clearances, construction, roadway constraints and site access.

    Request route survey

  • Oversize-load coordination

    Escort scheduling, staging and communication with your dispatcher and transport crew so the move runs to the permit’s times and conditions.

    Request oversize-load coordination
