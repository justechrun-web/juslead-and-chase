# Operational and regulatory review notes

This website is marketing. It is **not** a compliance determination, and nothing on it should be read as one.

## What the site does and doesn't claim

| Topic | Site wording | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operations base | Scranton, Pennsylvania | Stated in the brief |
| New York | "Service area. Escort certification is confirmed with each quote." | Certification is **operator-stated, not independently verified**. No certification claim or badge is made in copy |

The site also states that service areas are not statements of certification.

## Varies by jurisdiction — verify per state, per permit

- **Escort / pilot-car certification.** Which states require it, which reciprocity applies, and the training or renewal rules.
- **Warning lights.** Colour (amber), type (rotating, strobe or LED bar), mounting, visibility distance, and when they may be lit.
- **Signs.** "OVERSIZE LOAD" wording, sign dimensions, letter height, colours, mounting position (roof vs. bumper), and when signs must be covered or removed.
- **Flags.** Colour, minimum size, mounting height and number. The concept shows four flags; that is design, not a requirement.
- **High pole.** Height above the load, non-conductive material, and when a height pole is required.
- **Vehicle.** Minimum vehicle type, size and weight, required equipment (two-way radio, first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, cones, flares, stop/slow paddle, safety vest, flashlight, measuring device).
- **Window tint.** A matte-black unit with dark tint may conflict with state tint limits.
- **Magnets and markings.** Required company name or ID markings, size, and placement. Any "certified" marking must match a current credential in that state.
- **Insurance.** Minimum liability coverage required for escort vehicles in some states. Not mentioned on the site until documented.
- **Permit conditions.** Number and position of escorts, travel-time windows, routes, and police-escort thresholds set on each permit.

## Before claiming any credential on the site
1. Hold the current document (certificate, card or registration number) and know its expiry date.
2. Confirm it covers the work described (lead, chase, high pole).
3. Then change the copy, and keep a dated copy of the evidence.
